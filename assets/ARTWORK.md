## Per-page botanical collection

The fifteen additional plates are by Pierre-Joseph Redouté, from *Choix des plus belles fleurs : et des plus beaux fruits*, digitized by the Biodiversity Heritage Library / Missouri Botanical Garden, Peter H. Raven Library, and obtained through Wikimedia Commons. The underlying historical work and faithful scans are public domain; BHL also distributed digitizations under [CC BY 2.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/2.0/). Author, institution, individual source links, edition-date uncertainty, download URLs and checksums are retained in `botanicals/provenance-a.json` and `botanicals/provenance-b.json`. Some files are intact official Wikimedia thumbnails; the originals and download variants are distinguished in those records.

The website uses CSS masking, desaturation, blending, and motion to place these images in page margins. Local source image pixels are not edited. Artwork credits remain here in the source documentation rather than appearing as a content module in the interface. Home uses the anemone plate documented above.
